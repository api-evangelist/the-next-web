“We lose Starlink and lose the interceptor,” Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy told reporters on 17 October, Bloomberg reported. Zelenskyy said speed limits on Starshield, SpaceX’s military satellite network, remained in place across Ukraine. Ukraine’s interceptor drones lose the link above 161 km/h (100 mph), he said in audio messages on WhatsApp.
